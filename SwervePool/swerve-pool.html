How to play
  • Aim: drag across the table. Use ◀ ▶ for fine steps.
  • Tip: drag the blue dot on the white ball to strike off centre.
  • Cue lift: raise the butt of the cue. Side spin plus lift bends the path.
  • Shoot: pull the power strip down and let go.
  • Look around: drag with two fingers to turn the camera. Pinch to zoom.
  • Views: the view button steps through Cue, Top and Free. The Free view stays put when the ball is hit.
  • Place: on the break, or with ball in hand, drag the cue ball itself.
  • The game: UK 8-Ball. Pot all of your colour, then the black. Your colour is set by the first ball you legally pot.
  • Break: from behind the baulk line. Pot a ball or send four balls to a cushion.
  • Every shot: hit your own colour first, then pot a ball or reach a cushion.
  • Fouls: your opponent gets two visits. If the cue ball was potted, they also place it behind the baulk line.
  • The black: potted early or with a foul, it loses the frame. Potted on the break, the balls are racked again.
